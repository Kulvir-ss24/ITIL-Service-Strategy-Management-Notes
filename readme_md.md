This repository contains synthesized notes based on the ITIL Service Strategy curriculum (PG-DITISS). The notes cover fundamental concepts for IT Infrastructure and Data Center Management.

## Key Takeaways

* **Lifecycle Approach:** IT infrastructure management follows a distinct lifecycle: *Strategy -> Design -> Transition -> Operation*.
* **Utility & Warranty:** A service must be both fit for purpose (Utility) and fit for use (Warranty) to provide actual business value.
* **Provider Typology:** IT services can be sourced internally (Type I), shared across a corporation (Type II), or contracted to external vendors (Type III).
* **Demand Management:** Prevents system crashes and wasted budget by analyzing Patterns of Business Activity (PBAs) and User Profiles (UPs) to forecast resource needs.
* **Role Separation:** Distinct roles ensure accountability. For instance, Incident Management focuses on rapid restoration, while Problem Management investigates root causes.
